How it works

Prove. Bind. Enforce.

x402 tells an agent how to pay. A cap in your server only works if the agent goes through your server. The rules have to live where the money lives.

Prove one passport per agent

The owner generates a ZK passport credential in the browser, anti-Sybil by nullifier. It is verified onchain without revealing who the owner is.

Circom · Groth16 · BN254 verifier on Soroban

Bind one passport, one account

The factory verifies the proof and deploys the agent's own smart account in the same transaction. The owner sets a spend cap and the payees it may pay.

PassportFactory.register

Enforce every payment checked

Before a USDC transfer settles, the account checks a live passport, an allowed payee, the cap for the period and revocation. Otherwise the ledger refuses.

Enforced onchain

Spend cap per period, allowed payees, owner revocation and a live passport, all in the account's __check_auth. The official x402 facilitator rejects at simulation and the ledger rejects if anyone skips it.

Checked by the seller

“Only passport accounts may buy” is the API's own rule: it reads the factory onchain before serving. A token transfer cannot be refused by whoever receives it, so we say so plainly.

Known limits, said plainly: the prior passport validator does not restrict who can mint a passport, so today the passport is a ZK passport credential, one per agent, anti-Sybil by nullifier, not proof of a unique person. The payee list ships with the new account contract (wasm b9dfc7c6…); accounts created before it (wasm f5ec1e6d… and older) only have the cap. Our own review found a way for a third party to lock the first accounts; we fixed it for new accounts during the event. All limitations.
